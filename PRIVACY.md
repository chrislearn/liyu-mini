# LiYu Demo privacy policy (experimental draft)

LiYu Demo is an offline demonstration. It does not ask for an account, password, payment information, contacts, or a one-time code. It does not make network requests.

Gift-card recipient labels and messages, simulated gift state, and wishes are stored in the app's local storage area on the device. They are not transmitted to the publisher. Users can remove individual demo gifts and wishes within the app; deleting the app's data removes the remaining local records.

This policy is a draft for the experimental app. It becomes the live policy only after the publisher reviews it and publishes it at the URL shown in the listing. For questions about the demo, use the publisher's GitHub profile linked in the listing.
